Passive earning lets you earn weekly ASK rewards by opting into data sharing through the Permission Agent browser extension. Your browser activity, such as site visits and shopping behaviors, is privately tracked and structured. You earn based on the amount and type of opt-in data you share. Rewards are typically distributed on Mondays. You always have control and can opt out at any time. Would you like to know how to enable passive earning, or perhaps check your current ASK balance?
